Consider emp, dept and salgrade tables.

1. Display ename, sal, deptno, dname of all  grade 3 salesman. 
SELECT e.ename, e.sal, e.deptno, d.dname 
FROM emp e 
JOIN dept d ON e.deptno = d.deptno 
JOIN salgrade s ON e.sal BETWEEN s.losal AND s.hisal 
WHERE s.grade = 3 AND e.job = 'Salesman';

Assignment: 4

Use Banking database to solve below queries
1. Show **customer name, branch city, and account type**.
select c.name, b.city, a.account_type
from customer c
join branch b
on c.branch_id=b.branch_id
join account a
on c.customer_id=a.customer_id;

2. Show **loan details with customer and branch name**.
select l.loan_id, l.customer_id, l.loan_type, l.amount, l.status , b.branch_name
from customer c
join loan l
on c.customer_id = l.customer_id
join branch b
on c.branch_id = b.branch_id;

3. Display **customers who have accounts**.
select c.customer_id , c.name , c.phone , c.address , c.branch_id ,a.account_type
from customer c
left join account a
on c.customer_id = a.customer_id;

4. Display **customers who have taken loans**.
select c.customer_id , c.name , c.phone , c.address , c.branch_id ,l.loan_type
from customer c
left join loan l
on c.customer_id = l.customer_id;

5. Show **transaction amount with account type**.
select t.amount, a.account_type
from transactions t
join account a
on t.account_id=a.account_id;

6. Find **customer who has maximum account balance**.
select c.customer_id, c.name, a.balance
from customer
c join account a
on c.customer_id=a.customer_id
where balance = (select max(balance) from account);

7. Find accounts with **balance greater than average balance**.
select a1.*
from account a1
join account a2
group by a1.account_id, a1.customer_id, a1.account_type, a1.balance, a1.open_date
having a1.balance > avg(a2.balance);

8. Find customers who **have taken loans**.
select distinct c.*
from customer c
join loan l on c.customer_id = l.customer_id;

9. Display accounts that **have transactions**.
select distinct a.*
from account a
join transactions t on a.account_id = t.account_id;

10. Find customers who **do not have loans**.
select c.*
from customer c
left join loan l on c.customer_id = l.customer_id
where l.customer_id is null;
