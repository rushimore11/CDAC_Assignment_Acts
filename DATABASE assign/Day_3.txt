Grouping Data together (Use emp, dept and salgrade
table)

1.Find how much salary amount is invested in the employees
working as a salesman of the company.
SELECT job,sum(sal) as total_sal_salesman 
from emp
group by job
having job = 'salesman';

2. Find minimum and maximum salary earned by any employee working
as a clerk in the company.
SELECT job,max(sal) as max , min(sal) as min
 from emp
group by job
having job='clerk' ;

3. Find how many employees were hired in the year 1980.
SELECT YEAR(hiredate) as year, COUNT(*) as count
FROM emp 
group by YEAR(hiredate)
having year = '1980';

4. Display  sum of salary and minimum salary for all employees who
are managers.
select job,sum(sal) , min(sal)
from emp
group by job
having job = 'manager'; 


5. Find total salary for each dept where there is more than 1
employee working as a clerk .  Use emp table.
select deptno ,sum(sal) 
from emp
group by deptno
having count(case when job ='clerk' then 1 end);


6. Calculate how many employees earn sal more than 1500 in each
dept. Arrange the data according to the deptno in ascending
order.
select deptno,count(*) as count
from emp
where sal > 1500
group  by deptno
order by count desc;

7. List minimum salary for each department.
